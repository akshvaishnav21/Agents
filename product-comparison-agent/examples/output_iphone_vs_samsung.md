### iPhone 17 — Pros & Cons
**Pros:**
- Best single-core CPU performance on the market
- Seamless iOS ecosystem (AirDrop, Handoff, iMessage, AirPlay)
- Compact and lightweight for one-handed use
- Consistent software updates for 5+ years
- Superior app quality and privacy controls

**Cons:**
- 60Hz display feels dated at this price point
- No S Pen or stylus support
- Camera system not as versatile as Samsung's
- Limited customization compared to Android
- Starting storage of 128GB can fill up quickly

---

### Galaxy S26 Ultra — Pros & Cons
**Pros:**
- Incredible camera versatility — 200MP + 4 lenses
- Built-in S Pen for note-taking and precision tasks
- Large 6.9" 120Hz display, great for productivity
- More storage and RAM options
- Highly customizable Android experience

**Cons:**
- Premium price starts at $1,299 (+$500 over iPhone 17)
- Heavier and bulkier (237g vs 170g)
- Android updates and long-term support less consistent
- One UI can feel overwhelming to new users
- Battery life improvement requires adaptive charging habits

---

### Verdict

**Buy the iPhone 17 if:**
You're already in the Apple ecosystem, prefer a compact phone, value simplicity and long-term software support, or primarily use your phone for social media, calls, and casual photography.

**Buy the Galaxy S26 Ultra if:**
You want the best mobile camera system available, use your phone for professional photography or content creation, need the S Pen for productivity (note-taking, sketching, document signing), or want maximum customization from your Android experience.

**Bottom line:** At a $500 price difference, the S26 Ultra is for power users who will actually use its advanced features. For most people, the iPhone 17 delivers a smoother, more reliable experience at a lower price.
